How to read it

  • Scores are YouCam AI Skin Analysis ui_scores; higher is better.
  • The after photo is scanned twice: as it is, and with its light matched to the before photo. The difference is what the light alone produced.
  • A change counts only if it exceeds the noise band for that concern, measured on 3 faces (evidence).

What it does not establish

  • That the product caused, or did not cause, any change in skin.
  • Anything about different poses, makeup, shadows or cameras.
  • Anything outside the light gap measured; beyond it the verdict is withheld.
